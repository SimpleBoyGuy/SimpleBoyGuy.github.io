About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, advised by Prof. Jianfeng Mao.

My research focuses on reinforcement learning, data-driven optimization, and multi-agent systems for scheduling and other complex decision-making problems. I am particularly interested in flexible job shop scheduling under safety, scalability, and structure-awareness considerations.

Before my doctoral study, I received my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from CUHK-Shenzhen with first-class honors. I have also worked on smart transportation, crowdsensing, and sensing-driven intelligent systems at SRIBD and AIRS.

Recent Research Highlights

  • Action-priority policy optimization: reinforcement learning methods for flexible job shop scheduling with transparent action selection. Project overview →
  • Structure-aware scheduling: using problem structure and instance information to improve generalization across scheduling problems.
  • Safe and scalable decision-making: learning policies that respect feasibility constraints while remaining effective across problem sizes.
